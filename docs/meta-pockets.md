# Meta pockets: MIDI Drum Kit (US)

One pocket = one kind of real person, called out by name in the image. Meta reads the text in the image and the primary text and shows the ad to people who match, so each pocket is its own small audience. Run each one as its own ad set at a small budget: the pocket finds its buyers, a broad ad set would never spend there.

Rules for every callout:
- It names the person ("If you play rock but…", "Making trap for 10+ years but…"), then the problem the kit solves. Never the product name.
- **Meta's personal-attributes rule**: the ad must not say or imply the viewer's age, religion, health, money situation, race, sexuality or criminal record. "Over 30?" or "Struggling with money?" get rejected. Say it with the activity instead ("Started making beats in 2012?", "Play gospel?").
- Producer names are allowed as a reference. The primary text always adds "Not affiliated with [name]".
- Claims stay inside `grind-studio-landing-pages/docs/product-brief.md`: 1050 patterns, trap / drill / hip-hop, any DAW, rolls and 808s included, commercial license. Use the same image template as the two best recent ads (DK_0925_08c, DK_0925_09): Impact caps, DAW photo, orange bar "1050 DRUM MIDI PATTERNS. $27".
- How to read: pause a pocket once it spends €50 without a purchase. Once a pocket's cost per purchase is under €25 after €100, keep it on, then write 2–3 neighbouring pockets around it.

## How the image and copy work (agreed 2026-11-01)

- **Image**: pure black, the laptop with the piano roll blurred behind, the line dead centre in Bebas Neue caps with a few words in the accent colour, GRIND ◆ STUDIO at the top, "MIDI DRUM KIT · $27" at the bottom. Nothing else on the image. Template: `render-w1.mjs` in the session scratchpad.
- **The line** always opens with a question that names the person, then gives one honest piece of advice in normal spoken English, the way a producer friend would say it. No slogans, no hype words, no clipped copywriting.
- **Primary text**: the same line, then two plain sentences about the kit, then "$27, one payment. Works in any DAW." (plus "Not affiliated with…" where a brand or artist is named).
- **One flexible ad per pocket** with 6 images (1:1, 4:5, 9:16 × orange and green). Meta picks the format and colour per placement.
