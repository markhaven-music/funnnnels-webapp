[SHOW: title Why your trap hats sound like a metronome]

Why do your trap hi-hats sound like a metronome?

[DEMO: hats-flat]

[SHOW: hats flat]

Every hit in the same spot, at the same volume. Now the same beat, programmed the way the records do it.

[DEMO: hats-rolled]

[SHOW: hats rolled]

Three changes. Velocity: loud on the beat, softer in between, like a hand playing it.

Rolls: before the clap, the hats split into triplets and thirty-second notes.

And gaps. Drop a hit now and then, so the next roll lands harder.

[SHOW: hats both]

Same tempo, same sounds. Only the pattern changed.
